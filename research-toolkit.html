Cross-check a case

Before drawing conclusions, try to confirm the same event from independent primary sources.

  • CourtListener: opinions & available dockets
  • PACER: federal dockets
  • U.S. federal court directory
  • DOJ official announcements
  • 50-state court & lawyer research

Understand legal outcomes

Allegation: a claim not yet established by a court.

Criminal charge: an accusation; not a conviction.

Civil settlement: resolution that may contain no admission of liability.

Verdict or judgment: a court outcome; check appeals and post-judgment filings.

Dismissal: a case ended, sometimes without deciding the underlying facts.

Check publication dates and status

  • Identify the original publisher and date.
  • Differentiate an incident date from a filing date.
  • Check whether the case is open, appealed or final.
  • Read the actual court record when accessible.
  • Never infer that search-result ranking proves reliability.
