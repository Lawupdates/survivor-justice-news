Search official registered sex offender registries

Access the U.S. Department of Justice's Dru Sjodin National Sex Offender Public Website (NSOPW) to search public registration records nationwide, or select a specific jurisdiction. Its official search supports names and ZIP codes, and depending on the jurisdiction, city, county and address-based searches.

For safety and accuracy, you will complete the search on the official government website, including its required terms and CAPTCHA. Select your state there and enter your ZIP code. We do not copy, store, rank, or republish individuals' registry information.

Official nationwide sex offender registry search · FBI guide to sex offender registry websites

Registration does not establish that someone is currently committing a crime; likewise, absence from a public registry does not establish that a person has never committed an offense. Details, availability and restrictions vary by jurisdiction. Do not use this information to harass or threaten anyone.
